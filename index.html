Andhra Kraistava Keerthanalu is dedicated to preserving, documenting, and sharing the rich legacy of Telugu Christian hymnody. Our goal is to make these timeless hymns,hymn writers, historical context, books, interviews, and recordings accessible to believers around the world.

We believe these hymns are more than music—they are a testimony of faith, worship, and the spiritual heritage of Telugu-speaking Christians across generations.

About Andhra Kraistava Keerthanalu

Andhra Kraistava Keerthanalu is a digital archive dedicated to Telugu Christian hymns, their writers, lyrics, history, books, recordings, and spiritual heritage.

Cultural and Spiritual Significance

These hymns hold a special place in the hearts of Telugu-speaking Christians worldwide. They bridge the gap between ancient Christian traditions and local cultural expressions, creating a unique spiritual language that resonates with the Telugu community.

By preserving these hymns, we honor the legacy of faith passed down through generations and ensure that future generations can continue to draw strength and inspiration from these timeless treasures.

Each hymn tells a story—of faith tested, of hope renewed, of love divine. They serve as a source of comfort in times of sorrow, inspiration in moments of doubt, and celebration in seasons of joy.
