The Work

Preserving the stories behind the songs.

Alongside this website, the project includes the preparation of books such as Andhra Kraistava Keerthanalu – Ardamulu , documenting the meanings of Telugu Christian hymns together with information about their poets, history and background.

This work involves collecting information about hymn writers, researching their lives and ministries, finding historical material, recording details about the songs, and organising everything so that this heritage can be preserved for future generations.

A song may last a few minutes. Its story can last for generations.

Financial contributions can help this work continue, especially by supporting the research, preparation and printing of these books.
