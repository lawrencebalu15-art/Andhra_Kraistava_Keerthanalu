Our Purpose

More than songs — a heritage worth remembering.

Telugu Christian hymns carry a rich history of faith, worship, poetry and personal experience. Many of these songs have been passed from one generation to another, while the stories of the people who wrote them are often less known.

This work seeks to preserve that heritage by collecting information about the hymns, their meanings, their writers, and the history surrounding them.

The goal is to organise this information carefully so that it can remain available not only for the present generation, but also for those who come after us.

Remembering the songs also means remembering the people and stories behind them.

The Research

Collecting the stories behind the hymns.

An important part of this work is researching the people who wrote these songs. Information about hymn writers is being collected from available historical sources, books, personal accounts and people who know their stories.

The research includes details about poets, the history of their songs, the meaning of the hymns and the circumstances surrounding their creation and use.

This information is being organised so that the contribution of these writers to Telugu Christian worship can be remembered and studied.

Andhra Kraistava Keerthanalu – Ardamulu

One of the major parts of this work is the preparation of Andhra Kraistava Keerthanalu – Ardamulu .

The book work brings together the meanings of Telugu Christian hymns along with information about the poets who wrote them and the history connected with their songs.

The research and documentation continue as more information about the hymns and their writers is discovered and collected.
